# Code of Conduct

is-at-the.top is built for everyone.

Whether you're here to register a subdomain, contribute code, report a problem, or simply hang out with the community, you're expected to treat other people with respect.

## Be respectful

Please:

* Treat others with respect, even when you disagree.
* Keep discussions constructive and relevant.
* Give useful feedback without attacking people.
* Assume good intentions when something is unclear.
* Respect the decisions of maintainers and moderators.

You don't have to agree with someone to be respectful to them.

## Don't

The following behavior isn't welcome:

* Harassment, bullying, or personal attacks
* Hate speech or discrimination
* Threats or intimidation
* Deliberate disruption or spam
* Impersonation
* Sharing someone's private information without permission
* Sexual harassment or unwanted sexual content
* Malicious behavior toward the project or its community
* Any other behavior that would make the community unsafe or hostile

## GitHub

Please keep issues and pull requests relevant to the project.

Do not use issues or pull requests for spam, arguments, personal disputes, or harassment.

Registration requests should follow the registration rules rather than being used as a way to start discussions.

## Discord

The same expectations apply to the is-at-the.top Discord community.

Please don't ping staff members repeatedly or unnecessarily. If you have a question, ask it in the appropriate channel and give someone time to respond.

## Moderation

Maintainers and community moderators may take action when this Code of Conduct is violated.

Depending on the situation, this may include:

* Asking someone to stop
* Removing content
* Closing issues or pull requests
* Temporarily restricting participation
* Removing someone from the community
* Permanently banning someone from the project or community

Serious or repeated violations may result in immediate action.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, please report it privately to the is-at-the.top team.

For security vulnerabilities, please follow the [Security Policy](./SECURITY.md) instead.

For abuse involving an `.is-at-the.top` subdomain, please use the official [abuse reporting process](https://is-at-the.top/abuse).

## Final note

We're all here because we want to build something cool.

Be kind, use common sense, and remember that there's a person on the other side of the screen.

Thanks for helping keep is-at-the.top a welcoming place.
